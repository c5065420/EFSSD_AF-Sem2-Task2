Kick-Off Connect Its our job to get you to kick‑off on time.

What We Do?
Our platform allows you to post a trip, browse upcoming fixtures, book seats in a few clicks, and track bookings and passenger lists in real time.
Our Mission
We treat every trip as a ritual: standing shoulder-to-shoulder, singing until your throat is raw, and turning strangers into family for ninety minutes. Across the UK, we move you toward the next match and the next memory.
Your safety is our priority

We are committed to keeping you safe. We combine smart technology with trusted safety partners and continuously build systems that make match-day travel safer and simpler for everyone.
